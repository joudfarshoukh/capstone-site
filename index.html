01 — About the Project

Bridging the Communication Gap

Deaf and mute individuals rely on sign language, but most people don't understand it — creating barriers across education, healthcare, and daily life. Our system removes that barrier using AI and computer vision, with no gloves, no wearables, and no expensive hardware.

📷
Camera-Based Vision

A standard laptop camera captures hand gestures at 30fps with no physical contact or wearable hardware required — making it accessible to everyone.

🤖
YOLOv8 AI Model

A fine-tuned YOLOv8n model delivers real-time gesture detection at 90–92% accuracy under controlled conditions, trained on diverse ASL datasets with data augmentation.

📡
Wireless Output

Recognized gesture labels are transmitted wirelessly via Bluetooth (HC-05) to a custom Arduino-based unit that displays text on an LCD and speaks aloud through a speaker.

⚡
Low-Cost & Portable

Total hardware cost of $50–100 using commodity components — compared to $500+ for glove-based alternatives — making it viable for widespread deployment.
